How to adjust

Trend is falling steadily

Hold the plan if energy, training and hunger are fine.

Flat for 2–3 weeks

Check logging accuracy, steps and sleep first. Then trim 100–150 kcal.

Dizzy or exhausted

Eat more, not less, and speak to a doctor or dietitian.
